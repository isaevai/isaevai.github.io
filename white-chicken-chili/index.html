White Chicken Chili

42 g protein and 395 calories a bowl, ready in 35 minutes.

Ingredients

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

  • 2 tsp oil (10 g)
  • 1 onion (100 g)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 can (4 oz / 113 g) diced green chiles
  • 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt
  • 4 cups (1 l) chicken broth
  • 1 lb (450 g) boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 cans (15 oz) white beans, drained (480 g)
  • ½ cup (75 g) corn
  • ½ cup (120 g) Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup (30 g) shredded Monterey Jack or cheddar
  • juice of ½ lime, cilantro

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the chopped onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, green chiles and spices for 1 minute.
  2. Add the broth and the whole chicken breasts. Simmer for 15 minutes, then take out the chicken and shred it.
  3. Add the beans and corn. Mash about a third of the beans against the side of the pot to thicken the chili. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Return the chicken. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the yogurt and lime juice.
  5. Serve topped with cheese and cilantro.
