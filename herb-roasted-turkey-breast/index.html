Herb-Roasted Turkey Breast

41 g protein and 233 calories a serving. In the oven for about 1½ hours, and enough for 8.

233calories
41 gprotein
6 gfat
2 gcarbs

per serving

A boneless breast cooks much faster than a whole bird and is easy to slice.

Ingredients

Serves 8 · 1 hour 45 minutes, 15 of them active

  • 3 lb (1.36 kg) boneless turkey breast, skin on
  • 1 tbsp (14 g) butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary, thyme and sage, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 onion (150 g), cut into wedges
  • 1 cup (240 ml) chicken broth

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Pat the turkey dry.
  2. Mix the butter, oil, garlic, herbs, salt, pepper and lemon zest and rub all over the turkey, including under the skin.
  3. Put the onion and broth in a roasting pan and set the turkey on top.
  4. Roast for 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
  5. Rest for 15 minutes, covered loosely with foil, then slice. Spoon the pan juices over the slices.

Calories and macros are per serving, calculated from the raw weight of every ingredient (USDA FoodData Central).
