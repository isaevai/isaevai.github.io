Protein Overnight Oats for GLP-1

32 g protein and 371 calories a jar. Five minutes tonight, breakfast for two mornings.

371calories
32 gprotein
7 gfat
48 gcarbs

per serving

It's eaten cold, so it's easy on shot day. Make two jars at once.

Ingredients

Serves 2 · 5 minutes, then overnight in the fridge

  • 1 cup (80 g) rolled oats
  • ¾ cup (170 g) nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup (240 ml) 1 % milk
  • 1 scoop (30 g) vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tbsp (12 g) chia seeds
  • pinch of cinnamon
  • 1 cup (150 g) berries

Method

  1. Stir the oats, yogurt, milk, protein powder, chia and cinnamon together until there are no dry lumps.
  2. Divide between two jars and top with the berries.
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or at least 4 hours. Keeps for 3 days.

The protein powder is whey, about 24 g protein per 30 g scoop. Calories and macros are for one jar, calculated from USDA FoodData Central.
