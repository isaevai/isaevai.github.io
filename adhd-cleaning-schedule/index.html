Three levels in every zone

  • Minimum, 10 minutes. What you see in the open. Always done.
  • Hidden, 15 minutes more. Open but easy to miss: windowsills, baseboards, tops of cabinets, doors.
  • Closed A, B or C, 15 minutes more. Inside cabinets and drawers, split into three parts so each fits 15 minutes.

The ladder: 6 steps, then start again

  1. Minimum + hidden
  2. Minimum + closed A
  3. Minimum + hidden
  4. Minimum + closed B
  5. Minimum + hidden
  6. Minimum + closed C

The rule. Did only the minimum? The ladder doesn't move: next time in this zone, you do the same step. With one step a week, hidden surfaces get cleaned every 2 weeks and everything closed every 6 weeks.
